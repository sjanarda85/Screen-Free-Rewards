The idea

Three languages, one woven mark

Three interlaced rings, one for each language, pass over and under one another and meet around a four-point reward star. Each language sits in its own ring: S for English, स for Hindi (from स्क्रीन, screen) and प for Sanskrit (from पारितोषिक, reward). Time away from screens is rewarded across languages and across generations.
